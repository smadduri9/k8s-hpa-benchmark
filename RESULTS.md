### Finding 1. Flash latency and ready-pod time

Client p95 medians: fixed **510 ms**, tuned **400 ms**, stock **380 ms**, across **6 paired repetitions**. Tuned p95 was lower than fixed in 6/6; stock was lower in 6/6.

Exact two-sided Wilcoxon: fixed/tuned **p=0.031250**, fixed/stock **p=0.031250**, tuned/stock **p=0.093750**. These are unadjusted tests. The tuned/stock comparison does not establish equivalence or a benefit from tuning. Arm order was not randomized.

Median ready-pod hours: fixed **1.18556 (n=6)**, tuned **1.78361 (n=5)**, stock **2.23514 (n=6)**. Ratios of unrounded medians are **+50.445173%** tuned/fixed and **+88.530928%** stock/fixed. Ready-pod time measures ready replicas integrated over sampled time; it does not measure consumed CPU or billing.

[Exclusions policy](artifacts/v1.1/exclusions.csv): tuned flash rep-1 retains its latency measurement but is excluded from ready-pod time because its replica series spans beyond the benchmark window. ## Reproducibility

From the repository root, after installing the pinned tooling dependencies:

```bash
".venv/bin/python" -B artifacts/v1.1/verify.py
".venv/bin/python" -B -m unittest discover -s tests -p test_publication.py
".venv/bin/python" -B scripts/generate_publication_text.py --check
".venv/bin/python" -B scripts/generate_public_figures.py
```

The verifier reads only `artifacts/v1.1/` and also runs when that directory is copied out of the repository. It uses the standard library and has no network or cluster dependency. The general `analysis/aggregate_runs.py` remains unchanged; it does not apply this publication's exclusions and is not the v1.1 authority.

Summary CSVs and JSON are generated by the verifier. The bounded findings blocks in README, this document and Pages are generated by [scripts/generate_publication_text.py](scripts/generate_publication_text.py). The remainder of those documents is maintained separately.

Replica peaks use the inclusive `t0` to `t0 + 1080 seconds` window, even for the contaminated series. Ready-pod time integrates each left sample's ready count until the next sample, without filling either window edge. The contaminated series is excluded from this integral in full; it is not silently trimmed. Its historical process origin is not established by surviving evidence.

Calibration uses the last pre-saturation step's request rate and recorded median pod millicores in a linear model. It searches integer amplitudes for which the constant minimum is below, and the flash peak above, the declared HPA target. The largest feasible integer is selected. One sweep is retained; repeatability of this model calibration has not been demonstrated. Individual per-pod CPU readings underlying the recorded probe medians are `MISSING`.

## Trace-derived load shapes (Phase 4)

[Selection rule](docs/SHAPE_SELECTION.md) and [source notices](artifacts/v1.1/SOURCE_NOTICES.md) describe attribution and transformations. The bundle reproduces normalized plateaus from the five retained aggregate windows. It does not reproduce the full candidate search over the original datasets. Source Hurst estimates are selection context, not measurements of delivered arrivals.

These are closed-loop Locust user-count envelopes. Periodic source windows span 24 hours and are compressed into an 18-minute benchmark. Individual source requests and their arrival process are not replayed. Current runs use trace-derived shapes; historical synthetic shapes remain separate.

## Latency — two metrics, never merged

Locust's `Aggregated` percentiles describe client-observed response time across mixed `/` and `/cpu` requests, including failures. Each reported p95 median is the median of run-level percentile estimates, not a pooled percentile across all requests. `/health` and `/metrics` are not workload requests.

Prometheus's `/cpu` histogram describes in-handler service time. It excludes client connection time and queueing before the handler and cannot establish a client-observed latency SLO. It is not the source for the publication p95.

## Measurement limitations

- Small samples and fixed arm order limit inference. Exact signed-rank tests use midranks for tied absolute differences, remove zero differences, and are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. A nonsignificant tuned/stock result does not prove equal performance.
- The package records declared settings, not a complete attestation of every deployed configuration. Paired CPU platform inventories and historical warm-up CPU comparison sources are `MISSING`. No percentage CPU-performance difference or hardware cause is published.
- Ready-pod time is a sampled replica-time quantity. It is not CPU consumption, monetary cost or a billing estimate. The historical general-aggregator tuned median includes a contaminated series; use the publication verifier instead.
- No usable Phase 5 cold-start distribution is published. The [collector methodology and kind calibration](docs/COLD_START.md) do not establish a GKE decision-to-serving distribution.
- `active_requests` and histogram bucket columns are implemented in the current collector. Statements that all current metrics files lack them are obsolete. This minimal bundle omits Prometheus CSVs and does not verify their coverage or publish an SLO result. [Bucket semantics](docs/phase5-bucket-schema.md) explain why overlapping row increases must not be summed as exact run counts.
- The app's `cpu_utilization_pct` gauge uses system-wide `psutil.cpu_percent`, not metrics-server pod CPU used by HPA or the probe. No pod-utilization headline is derived from that gauge.
- Hashes detect inconsistencies within the package; they do not independently attest that collection occurred as described. Sanitized completion extracts retain private source hashes, but private logs are not public inputs.
